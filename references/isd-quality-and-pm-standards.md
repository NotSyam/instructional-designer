## 1. 48 DIAGNOSTIC NEEDS ANALYSIS INTAKE QUESTIONS
*(Adapted from ATD ISD PM Standards for Analysis Phase)*

To prevent "The Great Training Robbery" (designing training for non-training problems), the ISD team must gather diagnostic responses across 4 stakeholder perspectives:

### 1.1 Project Sponsor Intake (Strategic & Business Need)
1. What is the overarching business problem or organizational performance gap triggering this request?
2. What specific business metric (KPI) must improve, and by how much, within what timeframe?
3. What is the cost of doing nothing (financial, operational, compliance, reputation)?
4. What previous training or interventions have been attempted to address this issue, and what were the results?
5. Who has final sign-off authority on deliverables and budget (the single Accountable stakeholder)?
6. What are the hard constraints: budget cap, hard launch deadline, mandatory delivery platforms?
7. What non-training obstacles exist (e.g., broken software, conflicting KPIs, incentive misalignment)?
8. What does success look like 90 days after training rollout?
9. Is this training mandatory or voluntary? What is the expected completion consequence?
10. Are there regulatory, legal, or compliance requirements mandating specific content or audit trails?
11. What is the escalation path if SMEs or review gates are delayed?
12. What executive communication or kickoff endorsement will be provided?

### 1.2 Subject Matter Expert (SME) Intake (Technical Content & Behaviors)
13. What do top-performing employees do differently compared to struggling or average employees?
14. What are the 3 most common, costly, or dangerous mistakes novices make?
15. What prerequisite knowledge or skills must learners possess before entering this program?
16. What authentic scenarios, edge cases, or "war stories" illustrate the core dilemmas of this job?
17. What source documentation, manuals, SOPs, or job aids already exist and are fully up to date?
18. What terminology, acronyms, or internal jargon must be standardized or clarified?
19. What tasks require memorization vs. what tasks can be offloaded to a job aid or quick-reference card?
20. What is the step-by-step decision hierarchy an expert follows when troubleshooting an error?
21. What misconceptions do learners frequently bring into this subject area?
22. How has this workflow changed recently, and what changes are expected in the next 12 months?
23. What reference materials or job aids should learners keep at their desks?
24. What are the safety-critical, compliance-critical, or non-negotiable failure points?

### 1.3 Target Learner Intake (Audience Profile & Context)
25. Who are the learners: job titles, department, tenure, and prior educational/professional background?
26. What is the learner's emotional state regarding this topic (e.g., excited, skeptical, overwhelmed, anxious)?
27. What is the physical and digital learning environment (e.g., noisy floor, dual monitors, mobile only)?
28. What language, cultural, or reading comprehension factors must be accommodated?
29. What accessibility needs exist (screen readers, color-blindness, closed captioning, mobility)?
30. How much time per week can a learner realistically dedicate to learning without falling behind on KPIs?
31. What is the learner's immediate answer to: *"What is in it for me (WIIFM)?"*
32. What devices, browsers, and bandwidth constraints apply to the learner population?
33. How comfortable are learners with self-directed e-learning vs. instructor-led discussion?
34. What informal learning channels (Slack, peer chat, desk-side mentoring) do they currently use?
35. What incentives, recognition, or credentials will learners receive upon mastery?
36. What past training experiences have learners praised or criticized?

### 1.4 Instructional Systems Designer (ISD) Synthesis Intake
37. Does the performance gap stem from a lack of skill/knowledge, or a lack of motivation/tools/incentives?
38. What is the target Bloom's cognitive level for each required task?
39. What is the optimal delivery modality (ILT, VILT, e-Learning, Microlearning, Job Aid, Blended)?
40. How will mastery be evaluated (authentic performance scenario, portfolio, simulation, rubric)?
41. What is the estimated total instructional seat time required?
42. What authoring tools (Storyline, Rise, Video, Marp, LMS native) fit the technical constraints?
43. What xAPI / SCORM telemetry statements must be captured for compliance reporting?
44. How will the 4 phases of Merrill's First Principles (Activate -> Demo -> Apply -> Integrate) be sequenced?
45. How will cognitive load be managed (Sweller: Intrinsic, Extraneous, Germane)?
46. What spaced retrieval booster schedule (e.g., 3-7-21 days) will ensure long-term retention?
47. What Train-the-Trainer (TTT) materials are required for facilitators?
48. What is the pilot testing and iterative revision protocol?

## 2. 37-POINT ISD QUALITY AUDIT CHECKLIST
*(Adapted from ATD ISD Standards & Quality Assurance Rubrics)*

Use this checklist during **Gate 1 (IDD Review)** and **Gate 4 (Final Quality Audit)**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SECTION A: ALIGNMENT & NEEDS ANALYSIS (Items 1–7)                      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ ] 01. Training Needs Assessment Report is complete and approved.     │
│ [ ] 02. Business problem and measurable KPI goals are explicitly stated│
│ [ ] 03. Performance gap distinguishes training vs. non-training causes.│
│ [ ] 04. Target audience persona and prerequisites are documented.      │
│ [ ] 05. Scope boundaries (In-Scope vs. Out-of-Scope) are agreed.       │
│ [ ] 06. Legal, compliance, and safety requirements are identified.     │
│ [ ] 07. Single Accountable project sponsor is designated (RACI).       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SECTION B: LEARNING OBJECTIVES & STRATEGY (Items 8–15)                 │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ ] 08. Objectives use observable Bloom's action verbs (no "understand"│
│ [ ] 09. Objectives follow Robert Mager criteria (Condition+Verb+Standard│
│ [ ] 10. Objectives are explicitly tagged by cognitive/psychomotor level│
│ [ ] 11. Delivery modality is pedagogically justified by Bloom's level. │
│ [ ] 12. Adult learning theory (Andragogy) is utilized for adult cohorts│
│ [ ] 13. K-12 / Pediatric pedagogy is utilized for youth populations.   │
│ [ ] 14. Merrill's First Principles are reflected in core lesson flow.  │
│ [ ] 15. Keller's ARCS motivation model checks are passed.              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SECTION C: INSTRUCTIONAL ARCHITECTURE & FLOW (Items 16–23)             │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ ] 16. Content is sequenced in a logical order (Simple -> Complex).   │
│ [ ] 17. Content transitions smoothly from one module/topic to next.    │
│ [ ] 18. Cognitive Load Theory applied: extraneous decoration removed.  │
│ [ ] 19. Mayer's Multimedia principles applied (Dual-Coding, Signaling).│
│ [ ] 20. Apply+ objectives have concrete/direct practice (Dale's Cone). │
│ [ ] 21. At least one realistic branching scenario/simulation included.│
│ [ ] 22. Estimated seat time is realistic for cognitive endurance.      │
│ [ ] 23. Microlearning elements are properly scaffolded with boosters.  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SECTION D: ASSESSMENT & EVALUATION (Items 24–30)                       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ ] 24. Assessment items map 1-to-1 with stated learning objectives.   │
│ [ ] 25. Formative practice includes corrective, diagnostic feedback.   │
│ [ ] 26. Summative assessments measure authentic job performance.       │
│ [ ] 27. 4-tier analytic rubrics define explicit mastery standards.     │
│ [ ] 28. Safety-critical tasks contain Hard-Stop / Zero-Tolerance clause│
│ [ ] 29. Kirkpatrick Level 1 (Reaction) survey tool is drafted.         │
│ [ ] 30. Kirkpatrick Level 3 (Behavior) & Level 4 (Results) plan defined│
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SECTION E: TECHNICAL, ACCESSIBILITY, & PILOT (Items 31–37)             │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [ ] 31. WCAG 2.2 Level AA compliance verified (contrast, alt-text, kb).│
│ [ ] 32. SCORM / xAPI telemetry tracking statements are validated.      │
│ [ ] 33. Facilitator and Participant Guides are synchronized.           │
│ [ ] 34. Pilot testing with representative learner persona is conducted.│
│ [ ] 35. Pilot feedback log is analyzed and revisions executed.         │
│ [ ] 36. Train-the-Trainer (TTT) facilitation clinic is completed.      │
│ [ ] 37. Final acceptance sign-off is archived by Project Sponsor.      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. ATD DEVELOPMENT TIME RATIOS (ESTIMATION BENCHMARKS)
*(Hours of design/development effort required per 1 hour of finished training)*

Use these standard industry benchmarks (Defelice, ATD Research) for project timeline estimations:

| Modality & Complexity Level | Ratio (Hours : 1 Hour Finished) | Typical Description |
|---|:---:|---|
| **Instructor-Led Training (ILT) - Standard** | **30 : 1 – 40 : 1** | Slides, facilitator guide, participant workbook, handouts |
| **ILT - Complex / Custom Simulations** | **60 : 1 – 80 : 1** | Custom role-play kits, high-stakes lab exercises, video cues |
| **Virtual ILT (VILT)** | **40 : 1 – 60 : 1** | Producer scripts, breakout activities, polling, interactive digital whiteboards |
| **e-Learning Level 1 (Basic / Linear)** | **50 : 1 – 70 : 1** | Text, graphics, simple audio narration, basic multiple-choice quizzes |
| **e-Learning Level 2 (Interactive)** | **90 : 1 – 140 : 1** | Interactive scenarios, custom graphics, drag-and-drop, branched knowledge checks |
| **e-Learning Level 3 (Advanced / Branching)**| **180 : 1 – 250 : 1** | Complex decision-tree branching, gamification, software simulation with sandboxes |
| **Microlearning Bursts (5–10 mins)** | **20 : 1 – 40 : 1** | Single-concept scenario cards, short interactive videos, spaced retrieval boosters |
| **High-Fidelity Clinical / VR Simulation** | **300 : 1 – 500+ : 1**| 3D models, complex logic branching, physical-digital hardware integration |
