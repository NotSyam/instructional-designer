## 1. Instructional Treatment Table

| # | Content Chunk / Topic | Learning Event (Cennamo & Kalk / Gagné) | Treatment Ideas & Media Vision (What Learners See, Hear, and Do) | Reviewer / SME Comments |
|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| **1.0** | **Session Introduction** | Focus on Goals | • **Visual**: Split-screen opening video featuring a realistic workplace challenge.<br>• **Audio**: Narrative voiceover establishing urgency and stakes.<br>• **Action**: Learner sets personal learning goals before starting. | |
| **2.0** | **Prerequisite Diagnostic** | Connect to Prior Knowledge | • **Visual**: Interactive 3-item self-diagnostic check.<br>• **Action**: Drag-and-drop categorization of prior concepts with immediate feedback.<br>• **Remediation**: Optional review link for learners needing refresher. | |
| **3.0** | **Core Concept Demonstration** | Gain & Integrate Content Knowledge | • **Visual**: Diagrammatic animation showing internal mechanisms.<br>• **Media**: Worked example walkthrough with audio thinking-aloud modeling by expert.<br>• **Action**: Interactive slider allowing learner to manipulate variables and observe outcomes. | |
| **4.0** | **Guided Practice with Feedback** | Take Action & Monitor Learning | • **Activity**: Branching scenario with 3 decision choices.<br>• **Feedback**: Natural consequence feedback showing real-world impact of the choice.<br>• **Support**: On-demand 'Ask the Expert' hint box. | |
| **5.0** | **Synthesis & Reflection** | Synthesize & Evaluate | • **Activity**: Synthesis matrix where learners build a summary mind-map or action plan.<br>• **Assessment**: Scored criterion-referenced checkpoint. | |
| **6.0** | **Transfer to Workplace** | Extend & Transfer | • **Job Aid**: Downloadable 1-page quick-reference decision flowchart (PDF).<br>• **Action**: 7-day on-the-job micro-commitment reflection card. | |

## 3. Definition of Done (DoD) for Treatment
- [ ] **Content Completeness**: All essential subskills from the design document are mapped into chunks.
- [ ] **SME Alignment**: SME has reviewed treatment ideas and verified factual accuracy of procedures.
- [ ] **Feasibility Verified**: Media producer and developer have confirmed that proposed animations/interactions are within schedule and budget.
- [ ] **Client Approval**: Client has signed off on the creative treatment before storyboarding begins.
