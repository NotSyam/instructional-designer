## 2. ISD QUALITY & DESIGN READINESS CHECKLIST (35-POINT AUDIT)

Before any course is released for pilot testing or final deployment, the Lead ISD and Project Sponsor must audit against this checklist:

### A. Analysis & Alignment Gates
- [ ] **Needs Assessment**: Training addresses a verified performance gap, not a non-training policy/system issue.
- [ ] **Audience Profile**: Learner persona characteristics, environment, and constraints are explicitly defined.
- [ ] **Business Metric**: Quantifiable business impact KPI is identified and approved by Sponsor.

### B. Learning Objectives & Pedagogical Design Gates
- [ ] **Observable Action Verbs**: All objectives use measurable Bloom's verbs (no "understand", "know", "learn").
- [ ] **Mager 3-Part Standard**: Objectives define Performance, Conditions, and Criteria where appropriate.
- [ ] **Modality Match**: Delivery mode (ILT, VILT, eLearning, Blended) matches the cognitive demand of objectives.
- [ ] **Dale's Cone Alignment**: Apply-level objectives incorporate concrete practice (scenarios, simulations, drills).
- [ ] **Adult Learning Principles (Andragogy)**: Real-world relevance, prior experience recognition, and autonomy are built-in.
- [ ] **Gagné Sequencing**: Lesson flow follows attention -> objective -> recall -> presentation -> guidance -> practice -> feedback -> assessment -> transfer.

### C. Content & Engagement Quality Gates
- [ ] **Content Chunking**: Modules are segmented into digestible micro-units (<=20 minutes per module).
- [ ] **Cognitive Load Control**: Extraneous decorative fluff is eliminated; worked examples precede independent practice.
- [ ] **No Neuromyths**: Zero reliance on VARK learning styles or fake retention statistics.
- [ ] **Tone & Clarity**: Language is direct, professional, and free from unnecessary instructional jargon in learner materials.

### D. Assessment & Evaluation Gates
- [ ] **Formative Alignment**: Practice activities provide immediate explanatory feedback for correct AND incorrect choices.
- [ ] **Summative Validity**: Post-assessment tests match the exact cognitive level of the stated learning objectives.
- [ ] **Kirkpatrick Multi-Level Plan**: Instruments for Level 1 (Reaction), Level 2 (Learning), and Level 3 (Behavior) are created.

### E. Accessibility & Technical Compliance Gates
- [ ] **WCAG 2.2 AA Compliance**: Visual contrast >= 4.5:1, color-independent indicators, descriptive alt text for diagrams.
- [ ] **Multimedia Accessibility**: Synchronized closed captions and full text transcripts provided for audio/video.
- [ ] **Keyboard Navigability**: All interactions operable without a mouse; no keyboard focus traps.
- [ ] **LMS Interoperability**: SCORM 1.2 / 2004 or xAPI packages test clean in SCORM Cloud / target LMS sandbox.

## 3. CHANGE MANAGEMENT & SCOPE CONTROL PROTOCOL

To prevent *scope creep* and uncontrolled project delays, all change requests follow this process:

1. **Change Request Submission**: Stakeholder submits formal change via Change Request Form.
2. **Impact Assessment**: ISD analyzes impact on timeline (+Days), budget (+$), and learning architecture.
3. **Change Control Board (CCB) Review**: Sponsor, Lead ISD, and SME review and classify:
   - *Class 1 (Critical Defect / Regulatory Error)*: Mandatory immediate fix.
   - *Class 2 (Content Enhancement / Preference)*: Deferred to Post-Launch Version 1.1 iteration.
   - *Class 3 (Out-of-Scope New Module)*: Requires new project charter and separate timeline.
4. **Sign-off**: Approved changes update the baseline WBS and project schedule.
