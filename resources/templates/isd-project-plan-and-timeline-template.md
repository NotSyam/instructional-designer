## 2. RACI GOVERNANCE & COMMUNICATION PLAN

```
┌─────────────────────────────────┬───────────┬──────────┬──────────┬───────────┐
│ Deliverable / Decision Gate     │ Sponsor   │ Lead ISD │ Lead SME │ LMS Admin │
├─────────────────────────────────┼───────────┼──────────┼──────────┼───────────┤
│ Training Needs Assessment       │ Accountable│Responsible│ Consulted│ Informed  │
│ Instructional Design Doc (IDD)  │ Accountable│Responsible│ Consulted│ Informed  │
│ Storyboards & Content Accuracy  │ Informed  │Responsible│ Accountable│Informed  │
│ Media & Interactive Prototype   │ Informed  │Responsible│ Consulted│ Informed  │
│ LMS SCORM / xAPI Integration    │ Informed  │ Consulted│ Informed │Accountable│
│ Final Release Sign-Off (Go/No-Go│ Accountable│ Consulted│ Consulted│ Informed  │
└─────────────────────────────────┴───────────┴──────────┴──────────┴───────────┘
```
* **Responsible (R)**: The doer of the task.
* **Accountable (A)**: The final decision-maker / sign-off authority (only 1 'A' per deliverable).
* **Consulted (C)**: Two-way input providers (SMEs, Legal, Frontline Managers).
* **Informed (I)**: One-way progress receivers.

## 3. RISK MANAGEMENT & MITIGATION MATRIX

| Risk Identifier | Potential Risk Event | Probability (1-4) | Impact (1-4) | Risk Score | Mitigation & Contingency Strategy |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| **RSK-01** | SME Unavailability / Delayed Storyboard Review | 3 (Likely) | 3 (Major) | **9 (High)** | Establish 48-hour SLA in project kickoff; designate backup secondary SME. |
| **RSK-02** | Scope Creep: Unplanned Module Additions | 3 (Likely) | 3 (Major) | **9 (High)** | Enforce strict Change Management protocol; additional topics defer to v2.0. |
| **RSK-03** | LMS SCORM Tracking Failure / xAPI Drops | 2 (Moderate)| 4 (Critical)| **8 (High)** | Conduct Sandbox LMS upload test at Week 11 with Alpha prototype. |
| **RSK-04** | Pilot Cohort Non-Representative of Real Learners| 2 (Moderate)| 2 (Minor) | **4 (Low)** | Pre-screen pilot participants against the Learner Persona criteria. |
