## 3. Facilitation Tips & Troubleshooting
* **If participants are quiet during discussions**: Pivot to individual 1-minute write-first exercise before asking for sharing.
* **If time runs short in Practice**: Cut round 2 debrief short and prioritize full-group synthesis of core friction points.
* **Key Pitfall to Guard Against**: Do not lecture during the practice block; keep facilitator talk time under 30% of total session time.
