# Third-Party Framework Attribution & Disclaimers

This repository contains educational templates, architectural patterns, and automation tools inspired by established research and methodologies in the field of Instructional Design and Learning Sciences.

To ensure full transparency and respect intellectual property rights, the seminal works and trademarks that informed this project are acknowledged below:

## Frameworks & Trademarks Attribution

1. **Understanding by Design (UbD)**:
   * **Authors**: Grant Wiggins & Jay McTighe
   * **Publisher / Rights Holder**: Association for Supervision and Curriculum Development (ASCD)
   * **Attribution**: The concepts of Backward Design, Enduring Understandings, Essential Questions, and GRASPS assessment design referenced in this repository are adapted from Understanding by Design (Wiggins & McTighe, 2005). UbD is a registered trademark of ASCD.

2. **Action Mapping**:
   * **Author**: Cathy Moore
   * **Attribution**: The performance-first visual design approach (Business Goal -> Observable Behaviors -> Practice Scenarios -> Minimal Information) is adapted from Map It: The hands-on guide to strategic training design (Moore, 2017).

3. **The Systematic Design of Instruction (Dick & Carey Model)**:
   * **Authors**: Walter Dick, Lou Carey, and James O. Carey
   * **Publisher**: Pearson
   * **Attribution**: Hierarchical task analysis, subordinate skill analysis, and criterion-referenced testing structures are derived from Dick, Carey & Carey (2014).

4. **Successive Approximation Model (SAM)**:
   * **Author**: Dr. Michael Allen
   * **Publisher**: Allen Interactions / ATD Press
   * **Attribution**: The Savvy Start, Iterative Prototyping loops (Alpha, Beta, Gold), and agile instructional design workflows are based on Leaving ADDIE for SAM (Allen, 2012).

5. **Four-Component Instructional Design (4C/ID)**:
   * **Authors**: Jeroen J. G. van Merrienboer & Paul A. Kirschner
   * **Publisher**: Routledge
   * **Attribution**: The 4-component complex learning architecture (Learning Tasks, Supportive Information, Procedural Information, Part-Task Practice) is derived from Ten Steps to Complex Learning (van Merrienboer & Kirschner, 2017).

6. **Universal Design for Learning (UDL)**:
   * **Organization**: CAST (Center for Applied Special Technology)
   * **Attribution**: The 3 UDL principles (Multiple Means of Engagement, Representation, and Action & Expression) are adapted from the CAST UDL Guidelines (2018).

7. **Project Management Standards & ISD Checklists**:
   * **Organization**: Association for Talent Development (ATD)
   * **Attribution**: Project management timelines, WBS tasks, and quality checklists are adapted from professional practice guidelines published by ATD.
